The problem

People already bet on outcomes.
The venues do not fit.

Offshore and custodial

Funds sit with an operator, not in a contract you can verify.

Hard to reach

A Bitcoin-native user needs a separate chain, a bridge, and a stablecoin they do not hold.

Opaque

A proprietary order book and a private risk engine. You cannot check that a market is solvent.

No Bitcoin rails

Bitcoin has the money, the users and the finality — and no venue for collective opinion.

The solution

A prediction market protocol that runs
natively on Mezo.

  • Any wallet can create a yes/no market in one transaction.
  • An on-chain constant-product AMM prices the two sides and settles every trade.
  • The winner redeems 1:1 for MUSD, a Bitcoin-backed stablecoin.
  • Everything is verifiable on-chain: no operator, no order book, no off-chain component.

How the AMM works

The quote is the price you pay.

buy YES with a MUSD
  └─► split a into a complete set: a YES + a NO
    ├─ the NO half becomes counter-collateral
    └─ the pool releases YES (k preserved)

buyer receives  a + swapOut YES shares
priceYes = reserveNo / (reserveYes + reserveNo)
  • Exact solvency — every MUSD deposited mints exactly one YES and one NO.
  • Honest pricing — the marginal cost is the quoted cost, plus ordinary slippage.
  • The spread is the business — the liquidity provider earns it, openly.
