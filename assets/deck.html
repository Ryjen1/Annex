AMM & Fee Economics

Honest pricing, clean revenue.

buyShares YES with gross MUSD
  ├─ 0.30% fee (30 bps) ──► accruedFees (owner claim)
  └─ net splits into complete set: net YES + net NO
       ├─ NO half becomes counter-collateral
       └─ pool releases YES (k preserved)

buyer receives  net + swapOut YES · winner redeems 1:1
  • Exact collateralization — every net MUSD mints 1 YES + 1 NO.
  • Entry fee, 0% exit fee — winning shares always redeem at 1.00 MUSD par.
  • LP spreads preserved — creator LPs earn pool trade spreads at settlement.

Solvency & Yield Safety

The invariant is the product.

MUSD.balanceOf(this) == totalLockedCollateral + accruedFees  |  locked == totalYes == totalNo

Strict Fee Isolation

claimFees() withdraws accruedFees only — locked market collateral can never be touched.

Oracle & Yield Guards

Manual resolves revert on PriceFeed markets. Idle StabilityPool yield is deferred so withdrawals never lock.

65 Foundry Tests

51 Wave 1 regression + invariant tests plus 14 Wave 2 PriceFeed, fee isolation, Trove & fuzz tests.

Idle-pool yield was evaluated and deliberately deferred until atomic sMUSD liquidity ensures 1:1 redemption liveness.
